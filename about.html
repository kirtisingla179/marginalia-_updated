About

A blog platform built by students, for students.

Why we started Marginalia

Marginalia began as a class project between two roommates who were tired of writing essays that only one professor would ever read. We wanted a place where the funny voice memo you left yourself at 1am, or the recipe you perfected in a dorm kitchen, could become something other students actually find and enjoy.

Today, writers on 340 campuses use Marginalia to publish essays, poetry, study guides, and everything in between — for free, forever, with no ads and no algorithm deciding what gets seen.

What we believe

Good writing doesn't need a huge audience to matter. It needs the right one. That's what we're building — a home for the stories that live in the margins of a real semester.
