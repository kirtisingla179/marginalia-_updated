Good to know

Privacy

Plain-language notes on what we collect and how your posts are shared.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your name, student email, and the posts you publish. We don't sell your data, and we don't run ads, so there's no tracking network feeding on your activity here.

Who can see your posts

You choose the audience for every post: campus-only, or public to the whole Marginalia network. You can change this at any time, and you can delete a post — or your whole account — whenever you like.

Questions

If you have questions about your data, reach out through our contact page.
